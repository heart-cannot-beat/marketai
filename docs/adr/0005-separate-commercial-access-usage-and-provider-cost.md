# Separate commercial access, usage, and provider cost

MarketAI will bill the first paid product as a fixed Workspace subscription while customers pay AI providers through BYOK, with free invite-only Beta access represented by a revocable Access Grant rather than a card-backed trial. The SaaS control plane owns immutable PlanVersions, Subscriptions, Access Grants, Restrictions, Billing Provider events, and the commercial Billing Ledger; FastAPI receives leased, versioned Entitlement Snapshots through a transactional outbox, then exclusively enforces domain capacity, records append-only Usage, and atomically reserves and settles a separate multi-currency Provider Cost Ledger. We reject user-scoped subscriptions, shared billing/cost balances, platform AI credits, synchronous Stripe checks, direct editing of effective entitlements, silent Mock fallback, and permanent trust in the last entitlement because they would conflate revenue with customer-paid cost, weaken tenant and revocation boundaries, or make duplicate and uncertain external calls impossible to reconcile.
