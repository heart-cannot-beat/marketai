# Enforce the Workspace identity boundary at the BFF and database

The SaaS control plane is the sole authority for users, Workspaces, memberships, invitations, and revocable sessions; its BFF calls the private FastAPI service with per-request, short-lived ES256 delegated-user tokens, while FastAPI derives tenant and role from verified claims and enforces its own policy. The domain service owns only a Workspace projection, append-only actor attribution, jobs, and KMS-envelope-encrypted per-Workspace credentials, and hosted Beta uses PostgreSQL tenant context plus RLS with narrowly separated API, dispatcher, and handler privileges. We rejected browser-held domain tokens, request-selected tenant IDs, shared control/domain persistence, global customer credentials, stateless authorization, platform impersonation, and fail-open paid side effects because each would weaken revocation, auditability, or tenant isolation.
